Version 3.7.2

Bug Fixes:
- Stopped falsely offering to reset the key
  reporting mode after every command when using
  Fish with oh-my-posh, which together emit a
  duplicate command-finished mark after
  re-enabling the keyboard protocol for the next
  prompt.
- Fixed a bug on macOS 26 where the tab bar
  became short and shifted down after entering
  and exiting Lion fullscreen, and where the
  tabs sat jammed against the top of the screen
  in fullscreen. Affects the Tahoe tab bar with
  tabs on top.
