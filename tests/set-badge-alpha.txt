Alpha palette-binding demo.
Badge color is bound to ANSI 1 (red) at 50% alpha via
SetColors=badge=i:1@0.5. The badge should be translucent.
Change your Color Preset or toggle Dark Mode and the badge
tracks ANSI 1 while staying half-transparent.
